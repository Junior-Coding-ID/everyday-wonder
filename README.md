One sentence, up to 200 characters, on a small moment of wonder in everyday life.

> A child on the bus waved at a construction crane, and the operator swung its enormous arm just enough to wave back.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the entry sentence to `entry.txt` in the output directory
(`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT` or `/output` by default).

Run it with:

```sh
node main.ts
```

## Entry

A child on the bus waved at a construction crane, and the operator swung its enormous arm just enough to wave back.
